Property Documentation

Approvals and property documentation

Terms every property buyer should know, explained in general terms.

DTCP approval

Planning approval from the Directorate of Town and Country Planning, relevant to many layouts outside metropolitan limits.

CMDA approval

Planning approval from the Chennai Metropolitan Development Authority, where the property falls under its jurisdiction.

RERA registration

Applicable to certain real estate projects under the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, where the law requires it.

Patta and land records

Revenue records showing ownership details and land classification.

Parent documents and title

The chain of earlier documents that establishes how the current owner holds the property, and its title verification.

Encumbrance Certificate (EC)

A record of registered transactions on a property over a period, used to check for registered encumbrances.

Important: Applicable planning and building approvals vary by property. Not every property requires every approval, and one approval does not replace other legal requirements. Athithya Property Solutions does not claim any registration or approval here on its own behalf. Approval and documentation requirements depend on the property type, its location and the regulations that apply. Documents and approvals should be checked for each individual property, with appropriate professional or legal assistance where needed. Nothing on this website is legal advice or a guarantee that any particular property is approved or legally clear.
