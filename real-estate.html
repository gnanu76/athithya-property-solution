Buyer Guidance

Property approval guidance

Before buying, check the records and approvals that apply to the specific property.

Planning approval

Check the applicable DTCP or CMDA approval for the layout or plot.

Title and parent documents

Have the ownership chain reviewed.

Patta and EC

Verify revenue records and the Encumbrance Certificate.

Note: RERA registration applies only to projects where the law requires it. We do not claim that every property is approved; each property must be verified individually with supporting documents.
